About Avernoth

Avernoth is a third person spell-casting rogue-like RPG with a retro look and an emphasis on combat. Use magical tomes to vanquish an onslaught of deviants all while trying to escape the mysterious prison of Avernoth. Upgrade spells, grow more powerful, and free yourself while learning to maximize your magical potential.

This project is inspired by the look of the PSX and the feel of the contemporary standard-setters of the rogue-like genre.

Project Specs

Team size: 22
Role: 2D Artist, Concept Artist, Character Designer, UI Designer
Duration: May 2025 – May 2026
Engine: Unreal Engine
Software: Aseprite & CLIP Studio Paint

Character Portraits

To deliver the narrative, Avernoth has dialogue sections to help get to know the characters and tell the story.
Portraits for each character were needed to differenciate the active speaker.
Six characters needed designs and I was responsible for three:

Reflection

Avernoth is one of the more extensive projects I've worked on, requiring lots of hands-on interation along side project leads for a look that satisfied the art style while maintaining efficient output. It also did not go without its challenges- sometimes leading to overhauling art to better match an ever-evolving style. I am glad to have had a hand in refining the look of the 2D assets in-game in every aspect. The work is fun and has helped me utilize my ability to its fullest potential.
